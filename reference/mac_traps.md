## OS Traps ($A000-$A0FF)

Bits 8-10 of an OS trap word are flags: bit 10 = ASYNC (file/device) or SYS
(memory), bit 9 = IMMED (Control/Status/KillIO), CLEAR (memory) or HFS (file),
bit 8 = A0 not preserved (the trap returns a result in A0).
