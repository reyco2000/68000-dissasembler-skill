# Macintosh Low Memory: Exception Vectors and System Globals

Generated by `python3 tools/macsyms.py --markdown lowmem` from `tools/macsyms.py`.
Covers the 64K ROM (Mac 128K/512K) and 128K ROM (Mac Plus) era. Names follow
Inside Macintosh / MPW SysEqu.a. The annotator parses single-address rows.
